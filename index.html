The catch: presale tickets are capped at 4 per person

To seat 8 together you and your friend each buy 4, each on your own Telstra Plus login, Ticketek account and card, and line the two blocks up. The steps below cover how.

Hunting order

Ticketek only shows which bays are D and C once the sale opens. With five seated price levels, C and D will almost certainly be in the upper level (bays 53–84). Typically D is the back rows, the far end and the side-stage bays, and C is the front rows and better-angled bays. Whatever colours Ticketek shows, work down this list and take the first bay in your price level with room for 8. Bays on opposite sides with the same position have the same view, so they share a step.

Upper level

  1. The closest upper bays that still face the stage. 64 and 82 have the better angle; 65 and 81 are a little closer but more side-on.
  2. 63 and 83 are angled with a full view of the stage and screens. 66 and 80 are close but look across at the stage from the side.
  3. Corner bays. Good angle, further back.
  4. Corners of the far end. Full view, long way back.
  5. Far end, facing the stage head-on. 57 is dead centre. These are the furthest seats in the house.
  6. Side-stage. Physically close, but part of the stage and screens will likely be blocked. Only if nothing else is left.

If any C or D shows up in the middle level

  1. Better than any upper seat. Grab these if they appear at your price.
  2. Still better than the best upper bays.
  3. Middle level far end. About as good as upper 63 and 66, and lower down.
  4. Side-stage, partial view.

Rows: within a bay, a lower row letter is closer to the front. The front row of the upper level sits behind a metal rail (visible in the venue's photos), so rows B to E are often the sweet spot. A front-half row in a step-2 bay beats a back row in a step-1 bay.

Game plan for 8 seats

  1. Before 9:45am, both of you: check you can sign in to Telstra Plus (your Telstra ID) and to Ticketek, and have your own card ready. The limit is per purchaser, so use separate accounts and cards; orders over the limit can be cancelled. Get on a call or keep a chat open.
  2. From 9:50am: open the Telstra Plus Pitbull page. At 10:00 hit "Buy tickets", sign in, and follow it through to Ticketek. Both of you join the queue. Don't refresh or close the queue window, or you lose your place.
  3. Whoever gets through first buys first. Choose D Reserve × 4. If Ticketek lets you pick from the seat map, go down the hunting order and find a row with 8 or more empty seats side by side. Take the 4 at one end of that run so you don't strand a single seat. If it only offers "best available", look at where the seats are and whether there's room beside them.
  4. Send the seats straight away with the message builder below. The second buyer goes to that bay and takes the 4 next to yours, or the same seat numbers in the row directly behind. Two rows of 4, one behind the other, still keeps the group together.
  5. Check out quickly. Ticketek holds seats in your cart only for a few minutes.
  6. Switch to C Reserve if D has no row with room for 8 (or 4 + 4 front and back) within about a minute, or if D is only left in the side-stage bays. If D is only the far end (53–61) and C has front rows in 63–66 or 80–83, C is worth the extra $320 for the group.
